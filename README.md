# Valentine's Day Spending Analysis

This analysis plots historical spending by gift category from 2010–2022
to help inform Valentine's Day inventory planning.

Data source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/joebeachcapital/valentines-day-consumer-data?resource=download

## Running the Script

Extract the ZIP and open the included .Rproj file in RStudio.
Open valentines.R and click Source.

The script saves myplot.png and displays an interactive chart
in the Viewer pane.


For next year, jewelry should be prioritized while expanding clothing and gift 
card inventory moderately. Flowers and candy should be maintained. This is a 
reflection of historical customer spending and current trends heading into this 
year. While jewelry took a slight drop in 2021, it saw an increase in 2022 to 
just over 45 dollars per person which is higher than any observed spending in 
the sample period. It is also the highest of any other category. As mentioned, 
there is some volatility that comes with historical spending for jewelry in 
recent years so we should increase its purchasing budget with caution instead 
of assuming that this recent increasing trend will continue. 
Clothing and gift cards show moderate and sustained growth. Clothing increased 
about 10 dollars per person between 2010 and 2022 while gift cards increased 
from just over 8 dollars to 17 dollars per person. Both continued to exceed 
the previous couple years in 2022 which shows expansion continuing. Flowers 
and candy have both remained relatively flat in spending over the period and 
greeting cards had the lowest spending. Overall spending has risen from 103 
dollars in 2010 to 175 dollars in 2022, however participation declined from 
60% to 53%. Evening-out recovered from 2021 to 2022 as the second highest 
spending behind jewelry. While this is not a physical category, it is worth 
considering gift cards for restaurants.
